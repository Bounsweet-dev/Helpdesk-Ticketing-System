How to Troubleshoot a Computer that Won't Boot

Hardware • Updated Sep 8, 2026 • 5 min read

Problem

The computer does not power on or fails to boot into Windows.

Possible Causes:
- Lose power connection
- Faulty RAM
- Storage Failure
- Operating system issue

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Check the power cable.
2. Verify that the computer is receiving power.
3. etc...

Resolution:
If the computer successfully boots after these steps,
verify that the issue does not occur again.
